THE PUBLISHED SHELF - Groove MIDI Dataset

Some drum patterns in this app (the "published" section of
the drummer's book) are adapted from the Groove MIDI
Dataset:

  Groove MIDI Dataset (groove-v1.0.0)
  (c) Google LLC
  https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptation note: the source performances (human drummers
recorded on a Roland TD-11 kit) were sliced to short
pattern windows, their onsets quantized to a 16th-note
grid, their velocities rescaled to this app's range, and
their drum limbs remapped to this app's kit voices. The
dataset's own license text and index are kept with the
project's source data.

the phone, the Toy Cabinet's Mallets tab borrows that
room's six instruments as pitched extras. The sounds
are the family's own shelf: the same 44 anchor

inside this app as assets (single recorded hits, one
file per bar - no performed lines), credited exactly
